The engineering practice

Make AI useful without making control ambiguous.

01 / Consequence

Start with the workflow that matters

Identify the decision, user, operational cost or risk that must improve before choosing a model or architecture.

02 / Boundaries

Constrain what the system can do

Design tool access, data flows, API contracts, source boundaries and human escalation before autonomy reaches a consequential action.

03 / Verification

Test behaviour, not just the happy path

Evaluate retrieval, structured output and end-to-end outcomes. Expose failure modes, cost and latency while keeping deterministic fallback available.

See the AI engineering profile →

04 / Production ownership

Stay accountable after the demo

Ship the architecture and code, define monitoring and recovery, document the decisions and leave the team able to operate the system.

The niche

When AI can act, its operating boundary becomes part of the product.

The hard part is not making a model respond. It is deciding what the system may access, what evidence it may trust, how success is measured, when it must stop and who owns recovery. That is the engineering work I specialise in.
