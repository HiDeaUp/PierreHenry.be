Where I create leverage

The model creates capability. The operating boundary creates trust.

01 / Product outcome

A useful idea needs a consequential workflow

Define who acts on the result, what must improve and what a wrong or missing answer would cost before selecting the model.

02 / Agent boundary

Autonomy needs an explicit operating envelope

Design tools, permissions, observable actions, rate limits, fallbacks and human escalation around the real workflow.

03 / Evidence path

Outputs need inspectable grounds

Connect models through retrieval, APIs and structured outputs while retaining source attribution and controlled access to systems of record.

04 / Production proof

The system needs to survive the unhappy path

Make evaluation, latency, estimated cost, failure modes, monitoring, recovery and operational ownership part of the design.

The engineering method transfers

The same approach applies when a product has consequential workflows, fragmented systems, sensitive data or costly operational failure.

A bounded first engagement

Test one important AI decision against real constraints.

Production Risk & Delivery Sprint

Over five business days and up to 15 hours, I clarify the product outcome, inspect the available evidence, map data and integration boundaries, and make the most important AI and delivery risks explicit.

The result is written technical direction, an evaluation and production path, a 60-minute handoff and, when useful, one bounded implementation or prototype.
