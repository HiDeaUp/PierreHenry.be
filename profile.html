01 / Approach

Engineering judgement, followed by working software.

I move between product questions and technical implementation because the expensive mistakes often happen between the two.

My experience spans enterprise automation in financial services, online-ordering reliability, enterprise SaaS, health software used by doctors, patients and pharmacies, and founder-led food technology. Industry is evidence of range. The common work is turning consequential workflows into software people can rely on.

I also create open-source software and technical education. That public work demonstrates long-term ownership, depth of practice and the ability to explain technical decisions clearly.

01

Start with the consequence

Connect the technical decision to the customer, risk, cost or operational outcome it needs to improve.

02

Keep AI boundaries visible

Make permissions, source data, evaluation, fallback and human escalation part of the system design.

03

Design for operation

Treat ownership, monitoring, failure states and recovery as product work, not finishing details.

04

Leave the system understandable

Use clear contracts, documentation and tested decisions so the next engineer can work without guesswork.
