A practical first engagement

Reduce uncertainty around one important delivery risk in five business days.

Production Risk & Delivery Sprint

Over five business days and up to 15 hours, I identify the workflow that matters, inspect the evidence, map the system boundaries and make the most important product and technical risks explicit.

The sprint ends with written technical direction, a production path, a 60-minute handoff and, when useful, one bounded implementation or prototype. If further delivery is justified, the technical plan makes the next scope clear.

Describe the risk
  • Product outcome and user workflow
  • System boundaries and data flow
  • Correctness and operational risk map
  • AI opportunities and verification points
  • Technical plan and production path
  • One bounded implementation or prototype, when useful
  • 60-minute handoff
