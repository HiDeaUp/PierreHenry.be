Where I can help

The difficult part is usually the whole product system.

01 / Product build

A health product needs to move from idea to production

Clarify the workflow, expose assumptions, choose the architecture and build a release that real users can evaluate.

02 / Reliability

A user-facing workflow cannot fail quietly

Trace failure modes across code, data, infrastructure and operations, then add the controls needed for people to rely on the system.

03 / AI delivery

The team needs useful AI, not a disconnected demo

Connect AI-assisted workflows to a real product outcome, with evaluation, review and human judgement at consequential decision points.

Make boundaries, data flows, risks and operational ownership clear, then test the direction through implementation.

A practical first engagement

Start small enough to learn, with a path to production.

A focused product and engineering assessment

We identify the workflow that matters, the users and operational constraints, the riskiest assumptions and the smallest useful delivery path. The output is a written technical direction and, when useful, a working thin slice that tests the decision.

This can lead into hands-on implementation when the problem and working relationship are a good fit.

Describe the problem
  • Product outcome and user workflow
  • System boundaries and data flow
  • Correctness and operational risks
  • AI opportunities and verification points
  • Build sequence and production path
