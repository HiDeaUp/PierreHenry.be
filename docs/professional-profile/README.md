# Professional positioning spine

## Ten-second message

Pierre-Henry turns product ideas into shipped, correctness-critical software. He works from product definition through architecture and delivery, using AI-native engineering workflows to shorten the path to production without giving up engineering judgement or accountability.

## Long-term association

Pierre-Henry should be known for strong software engineering, sound technical judgement, building useful products and solving difficult problems. The objective includes senior roles, customer conversations, technical advisory, speaking, collaboration and discovering repeated problems that could become scalable products.

Pierre-Henry Soria is a product-first software engineer with 12 years of experience across enterprise automation, banking, health tech, New Zealand product engineering and founder-led food tech. He combines end-to-end product ownership with software architecture, backend and cloud engineering, AI-assisted delivery and technical communication. His public evidence includes a 13-year open-source platform with more than 11,000 commits, a programming language, four software-engineering courses with more than 225,000 combined enrolments, a technical YouTube channel and a Substack.

## Recommended base label

**Senior / Lead Software Engineer | Product Engineering | Software Architecture | AI**

Use a narrower label for each application:

- AI product role: **AI Product Engineer | Software Architect**
- early-stage role: **Founding Engineer | Product and Platform Engineering**
- principal role: **Principal Software Engineer | Product, Architecture and AI**
- architecture role: **Software Architect | Solution Architect | Product Engineering**

The title changes. The evidence and positioning do not.

## Claims discipline

- Do not claim comparative speed without evidence. The profile should show an AI-native operating model and shipped outcomes, allowing the reader to infer speed.
- Do not add domain claims beyond the supplied health-tech product experience.
- Do not use health tech as the main identity.
- Describe security through the verified security-advisory automation work unless broader security-engineering evidence is added.
- Do not turn tool names into the value proposition. Agents, MCPs and reusable skills matter because they improve research, implementation, testing and documentation.
- Do not inflate activity metrics into product impact. More than 11,000 commits proves sustained authorship and maintenance. Add users, releases, downloads or adoption only when verified.
- Use exact employer names, official job titles and dates only after checking the source CV.
