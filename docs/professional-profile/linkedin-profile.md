## About

I turn product ideas into shipped, correctness-critical software.

I am a product-first software engineer with 12 years of experience across enterprise automation, banking, health tech, New Zealand product engineering and founder-led food tech. I currently build automation for a security advisory group at a major bank. Previously, I worked on platforms serving doctors, patients and pharmacies at a Sydney health-tech startup.

My work spans product definition, software architecture, backend and cloud engineering, implementation and delivery. I am most useful when a team has an important product idea, a difficult system boundary or a workflow that needs to become reliable software.

AI is part of my normal engineering toolchain. I orchestrate agents, MCPs and reusable skills across research, design, implementation, testing and documentation. The goal is shorter feedback loops and lower operational cost, with engineering judgement, review and accountability kept explicit.

I also build and teach in public. I created an open-source platform that I have maintained for 13 years through more than 11,000 commits. I authored a programming language and four software-engineering courses with more than 225,000 combined enrolments. I publish technical material through YouTube and Substack.

That work is part of my engineering practice. Building a system and explaining its trade-offs both require clear models, technical depth and attention to the user.

I am particularly interested in fintech, food tech, AI-enabled products and other teams shipping software where correctness matters.

Wellington is my primary market. I am open to Senior or Lead Software Engineer, AI Product Engineer, Founding Engineer, Principal Software Engineer and Software or Solution Architect roles in New Zealand, Australia and with remote teams.

I am also open to focused conversations where a company needs senior technical judgement, architecture with implementation, help turning an early product into production software, or a technical speaker and collaborator. The common requirement is a real problem, meaningful ownership and a clear outcome.
