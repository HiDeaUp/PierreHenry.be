# Consulting inbound process

This is the internal operating guide for the existing Production Risk & Delivery Sprint. The public page is `production-risk-sprint.html`. Do not advertise a default price. Every quote depends on the client's system, complexity, requirements, access and agreed work.

## Client journey

1. A visitor sees the sprint on the Consulting page and follows the link to the dedicated scope page.
2. They review the typical problems, deliverables, process and documented engineering evidence.
3. They use the prefilled enquiry email or contact `pierre@pierrehenry.dev` directly.
4. Review whether the request fits the service. If it does, offer a short introductory call to agree on goals and boundaries. If not, explain why or discuss a separately scoped engagement.
5. Following discovery, send a written quote based on complexity, scope, required access, deliverables and availability. Never commit to a price before understanding those factors.
6. Agree on proposal, terms, confidentiality, access, payment and start date before beginning paid work.
7. Deliver the work and hand over clear findings and next steps.
8. Ask for feedback, permission to reference the work and referrals where appropriate. Offer larger delivery or embedded leadership only if useful.

## What to track

Record the company, contact, source of enquiry, stated problem, suitable service, urgency, permissions to work together, date received, last reply, next action and stage. Suggested stages: New enquiry, Qualifying, Call booked, Proposal sent, Won, Lost, Not a fit. Use the existing email inbox and a simple private tracker initially rather than building a CRM.

## Proposal outline

- Client and contact
- Problem and desired outcome
- In-scope systems and access
- Deliverables and acceptance criteria
- Out-of-scope work
- Timing and dependencies
- Project-specific price and payment terms
- Assumptions, confidentiality and responsibilities
- Expiry date and next steps

## Distribution and proof

The dedicated page will not create demand by itself. Link it from useful technical posts, relevant case studies, a LinkedIn Featured entry and thoughtful referrals when appropriate. Publish only claims supported by public work or the CV. Track qualified enquiries and signed proposals rather than raw page views.

## Operational limitations

The live enquiry CTA currently opens a prefilled email. It does not submit a form, store contact details, book meetings, automatically follow up, process payments or confirm availability. Avoid claiming any of those capabilities. Publishing must wait until the pull request is reviewed and merged. Ensure NZ work rights permit the intended paid engagement before entering an independent consulting agreement.
