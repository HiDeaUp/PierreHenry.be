How it works

Know the process before we speak.

  1. Send the context. Briefly describe your product, the problem and what a good outcome would be.
  2. Confirm fit. We have a short introductory call to agree on the question, access and boundaries. The call is not a free audit.
  3. Complete the sprint. I inspect the relevant architecture, workflows and evidence within the agreed 15-hour scope.
  4. Receive the findings. You get written priorities and a handover call. Any further work is optional.

Before you enquire

A few practical questions.

How is the price determined?

There is no standard published price. After a short conversation, I provide a quote based on the complexity of your project, required access, agreed scope and deliverables. No work starts until we agree on the proposal.

Do I need an internal engineering team?

No, but there needs to be an existing product or system and enough technical context to review it. I can work with founders, engineers or an existing delivery partner.

Can you implement the recommendations?

Potentially. Implementation or ongoing fractional technical leadership is a separate engagement. You are free to use the findings with anyone.

Will you need access to our production environment?

Not necessarily. The review begins with available documents, architecture, logs or selected code. Access is agreed in advance and should be proportionate to the question being assessed. Do not email credentials or confidential source code.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I'm based in Wellington and work with New Zealand and Australian teams. We confirm practical meeting times and project availability before scheduling.
