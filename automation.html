The method

A better process before a faster process.

I do not automate a broken workflow unchanged. We remove unnecessary steps, assign each decision to the right kind of system, and make failure visible.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Find the repetitive workflow that consumes time, creates delays or introduces avoidable mistakes.

  2. 02

    Design

    Separate rules, AI-assisted interpretation and decisions that still need an accountable person.

  3. 03

    Build

    Implement the smallest reliable system using APIs, software, automation tools and AI where appropriate.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Compare the before-and-after workflow using the client’s own volume, time and outcome data.

The rule

Use deterministic software for known rules. Use AI for bounded interpretation. Keep accountable people in consequential decisions.

Why senior engineering matters

The automation is only useful if it survives reality.

A demo can skip the hard parts. A production workflow cannot. Every build considers the failure modes around the model, the APIs and the people operating it.

  • Authentication, authorisation and secrets
  • Validation and structured data
  • Idempotency and duplicate processing
  • Retries, rate limits and queues
  • Audit logs and observability
  • Failure recovery and human approval
  • Model error, latency and cost
  • Privacy, maintainability and lock-in

Good fit

Digital work that repeats, crosses systems and follows recognisable rules.

SaaS, healthtech, agencies, professional services and other digital businesses are a strong starting point—especially when people repeatedly move information between email, documents, spreadsheets, CRMs and internal tools.

For healthcare workflows, health information is treated as sensitive, consequential actions remain human-reviewed, and technical recommendations are not presented as legal or regulatory advice.
