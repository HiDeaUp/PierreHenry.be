Ways to work together

The right format depends on the problem, the evidence already available and the ownership it needs:

  • Production Risk & Delivery Sprint
    Five business days, up to 15 hours, to clarify the highest-value risk and, when useful, test the decision with a bounded implementation or prototype.
  • Scoped engineering delivery
    Continue into a larger production improvement with explicit acceptance evidence, documentation and a clear handover.
  • Embedded senior engineering
    Join the team for a defined period to lead difficult decisions, contribute directly to the code and strengthen delivery around the work.
  • Founding or early product build
    Turn an idea or validated problem into production software while keeping product and architecture decisions connected.
  • Senior or lead engineering role
    Own important product and system decisions as part of the team over the long term.

A practical starting point

When the right scope is not yet clear, begin with a Production Risk & Delivery Sprint. In five business days, I inspect the evidence, map the workflow and system boundaries, and make the risks and trade-offs explicit.

  • Product outcome and user workflow
  • System boundaries and data flow
  • Correctness and operational risk map
  • AI opportunities and verification points
  • Technical plan and production path
  • One bounded implementation or prototype, when useful
  • 60-minute handoff

Roles that fit

The strongest matches combine product ownership, architecture and implementation:

  • Senior or Lead Software Engineer
    Own difficult engineering problems while contributing directly to the production system.
  • AI Product Engineer
    Build AI into a useful product or use AI-native engineering workflows to ship the wider product faster.
  • Agentic Systems Architect
    Design and orchestrate AI-agent systems, tools and controls while remaining hands-on with architecture and implementation.
  • Founding Engineer
    Turn an early product idea into a production system while making the architecture and delivery trade-offs visible.
  • Principal Software Engineer
    Own difficult technical decisions and stay close enough to the code to make the direction real.
  • Software Architect or Solution Architect
    Design system boundaries, integrations and delivery paths that account for product and operational constraints.

Staff Software Engineer, Solution Engineer, AI Platform Engineer and Developer Advocate roles are also relevant when they retain meaningful hands-on technical work.

Working style

  1. Start with the outcome. Define what must change for users, the business and the system.
  2. Expose the important decisions. Write down boundaries, risks, assumptions and evidence.
  3. Build through the uncertainty. Use implementation to test the architecture early.
  4. Automate with controls. Use AI agents and tooling where they shorten feedback loops, then verify the result.
  5. Leave the team stronger. Document the system, explain the decisions and share the method.
